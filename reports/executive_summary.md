# SkyCity DineMetrics: Executive Summary
### *Order Channel Performance Benchmark for Auckland Restaurants*

**Audience:** Executive Leadership, Franchise Owners, Commercial Stakeholders  
**Scope:** 1,696 Greater Auckland Restaurant & Bar Establishments  
**Reference Period:** September 2026 Snapshot  

---

## 📌 Headline Insights

1. **Massive Market Scale:**  
   Across 1,696 Auckland restaurant branches, monthly volume reaches **2,019,134 orders** generating **$77.7M in monthly revenue** and **$7.87M in total net profit** (10.12% industry margin).

2. **The "Aggregator Paradox":**  
   - **Delivery Aggregators (Uber Eats & DoorDash)** control **61.2% of all order volume** ($47.6M gross revenue).
   - However, due to 28%–33% commission rates, aggregators generate **only 5.2% ($409,610) of total industry net profit**.
   - Net profit per order on Uber Eats is **$0.32** (0.84% margin) and DoorDash is **$0.35** (0.90% margin).

3. **In-Store & Self-Delivery Drive 94.8% of Profitability:**  
   - **In-Store dining** generates **$10.31 net profit per order** (26.8% margin).
   - **Self-Delivery** generates **$8.82 net profit per order** (22.9% margin).
   - Combined, direct channels account for **$7.46M of the $7.87M total monthly profit**, despite driving only 38.8% of order volume.

4. **Widespread Dependency Exposure:**  
   - **35.6% of restaurants (603 branches)** have $\ge 70\%$ order reliance on delivery aggregators, leaving them highly vulnerable to commission fee hikes or algorithm adjustments.

## 🎯 4 Strategic Executive Recommendations

### 1. Build & Promote Direct Ordering (Self-Delivery)
* **Rationale:** Converting an order from Uber Eats to direct Self-Delivery increases net profit per order by **+2,656%** (from $0.32 to $8.82).
* **Action:** Invest in proprietary ordering web/app channels, offering direct order discounts or loyalty points that surpass third-party platform perks.

### 2. Differentiate Third-Party Menu Pricing
* **Rationale:** Aggregator commission rates (28%–33%) absorb almost all restaurant gross margin.
* **Action:** Apply a calibrated 15%–20% menu markup across third-party aggregator menus to protect unit margins and steer price-sensitive consumers to direct channels.

### 3. Convert Delivery Diners into In-Store Guests
* **Rationale:** In-store dining delivers the highest return per ticket ($10.31 profit / 26.8% margin).
* **Action:** Include personalized bouncing promotions (e.g. "$10 off your next dine-in visit") in every delivery package.

### 4. Continuous Channel Risk Monitoring
* **Rationale:** 603 branches are operating in the danger zone ($\ge 70\%$ aggregator reliance).
* **Action:** Utilize the interactive **SkyCity Channel Analytics Dashboard** to track real-time channel diversification and proactively assist underperforming franchise locations.
